Painted titles: readability options

Each card shows the Home and About titles on the real page white. The score is measured, not guessed: the share of each title's letter area whose colour is under 3:1 contrast against the page (the WCAG floor for large text), and the median contrast. Lower % is easier to read. Edge shadows help the eye but aren't counted. Hover the cards marked "hover"; "replay sweeps" replays the load animations. The gold glint and cursor light sit on top of whichever you pick.
